About

I am in the stage where questions are becoming a research agenda.

I am an early-stage PhD researcher and Junior Research Fellow at IIT Ropar. My current research direction is shaped by human-AI systems, Multi-Armed Bandits, Dueling Bandits, Online Learning, Preference Learning, Sequential Decision Making, and Learning Theory.

Before this stage, I worked as a Data Engineer at CloudEQ and built projects in machine learning, computer vision, dashboards, and recommendation systems. I do not yet list formal publications here; this site highlights research in progress, archived notes, projects, and preparation for future papers.

Current focus: integrating human expertise with AI, turning early bandit/preference-learning ideas into precise problems, and building reproducible experiments for future manuscripts.

Research Interests

How should an algorithm learn while it is still making decisions?

Bandit and online learning problems study the tension between trying uncertain actions, using what already looks best, and learning from limited feedback.

Multi-Armed Bandits

Choose, observe, update.

Each action gives feedback only for the option selected.

Exploration vs Exploitation

Try something uncertain or use the current best?

Move the control to see the policy shift.

Dueling Bandits

Sometimes feedback is a comparison, not a score.

Pairwise preferences help learn which option is better.
