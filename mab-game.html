Why It Matters

This tiny game is a small window into real research questions.

Explore

Curiosity has a cost.

Trying an uncertain arm teaches you something, but it might give fewer rewards now. This is why exploration is useful and annoying in exactly the academically productive way.

Exploit

Confidence is useful, but suspicious.

Once an arm looks good, exploiting it can increase reward. But if you commit too early, a better arm may remain quietly under-tested.

Regret

Regret is the bill for uncertainty.

Regret compares your choices with the best hidden arm. It is not here to judge you. It is here to make the theorem interesting.
