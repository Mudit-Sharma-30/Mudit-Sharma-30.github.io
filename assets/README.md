# Asset folders

- `profile/`: portraits currently used by the homepage and About section.
- `lab/`: laboratory, research-group, and workspace photographs.
- `travel/`: travel and location photographs.
- `conferences/`: conference, workshop, poster, and event photographs.
- `sports/`: sports and fitness photographs.
- `personal/`: hobbies, friends, pets, and personal-interest photographs.
- `research/`: research illustrations and general research imagery.
- `placeholders/`: temporary collaborator and content placeholders.

Use short descriptive filenames such as `neurips-2027-poster.jpg` or
`lab-meeting-spring-2028.jpg`. Update the corresponding HTML path after adding
or renaming an image.
